# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard | Excel & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a Sales Analytics Dashboard built using *Microsoft Excel and Power BI*.

Excel was used to prepare and organize the sales data, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard for analyzing sales, profit, orders, products, categories, and customer segments.

The dashboard helps to understand overall sales performance and identify important trends through interactive filters and visualizations.

---


## 🛠️ Key Skills & Tools Used

- *Excel*: Data preparation, data cleaning, and organizing sales data.
- *Power BI*: Interactive dashboard creation and data visualization.
- *Power Query*: Data cleaning and transformation.
- *DAX*: Created measures for Orders, Sales, Profit, and Profit Margin %.
- *Sales Analysis*: Analyzed monthly sales, product performance, categories, and customer segments.
- *Data Visualization*: Used KPI cards, charts, tables, and interactive slicers.

---

## Key Findings & Insights

- Total Sales reached **1.41M**.
- Total Profit reached **242.84K**.
- Overall Profit Margin was **17.2%**.
- **Electronics** recorded the highest sales among the displayed categories.
- **Consumer** segment contributed the largest share of sales.
- Top 5 products were analyzed based on **Sales, Profit, and Margin %**.
- Monthly sales trends were analyzed to understand performance throughout the year.

---

## KPI Analysis

- **Orders**: Tracks total orders with Month-over-Month comparison.
- **Sales**: Measures total revenue and compares performance with the previous month.
- **Profit**: Tracks total profit and monthly performance changes.
- **Profit Margin %**: Measures profitability as a percentage of total sales.

---

## Trend & Performance Analysis

- **Monthly Sales Trend**: Analyzed sales performance across January to December.
- **Category Analysis**: Compared sales across Electronics, Furniture, Office Supplies, and Clothing.
- **Product Analysis**: Identified the Top 5 products based on sales performance.
- **Segment Analysis**: Compared sales contribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- **Payment Analysis**: Included Card, Cash, and UPI payment modes for interactive analysis.
